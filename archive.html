Creative Philosophy

Visual Storytelling

Every photograph tells a story. I believe in the power of visual narrative to capture moments, emotions, and experiences that words cannot express.

Human Connection

Photography is about connecting with people and capturing the essence of human experience, whether through portraiture or documentary work.

Social Impact

From documenting the Fees Must Fall protests to capturing everyday life, I believe photography can drive social change and preserve important moments in history.

Continuous Learning

Photography is a journey of constant learning and growth, from technical skills to creative vision, always evolving and adapting to new challenges.
