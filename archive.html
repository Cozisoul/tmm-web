[Photography Journey]

[2014-2016]

Early Years

Began photography journey in high school, focusing on automotive photography with my cousin, learning the fundamentals of composition and technical skills. Evolved from automotive photography to portraiture, developing skills in capturing human emotion and connection.

[2016-2018]

University & Photojournalism

Joined university newspaper and documented the historic "Fees Must Fall" protests, learning the importance of storytelling through photography. As Image Editor, I was responsible for the selection, curation, and technical quality of all photographic content for the University of Cape Town's official student newspaper.

[2017-2024]

Professional Development

Expanded into multiple photography genres, built portfolio across platforms, and gained recognition on PhotoVogue. Developed transdisciplinary practice blending art, design, and technology within a single, unified practice.

[Creative Philosophy]

Visual Storytelling

Every photograph tells a story. I believe in the power of visual narrative to capture moments, emotions, and experiences that words cannot express.

Human Connection

Photography is about connecting with people and capturing the essence of human experience, whether through portraiture or documentary work.

Social Impact

From documenting the Fees Must Fall protests to capturing everyday life, I believe photography can drive social change and preserve important moments in history.

Continuous Learning

Photography is a journey of constant learning and growth, from technical skills to creative vision, always evolving and adapting to new challenges.
