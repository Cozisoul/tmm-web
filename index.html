About

I am a transdisciplinary artist and photographer based in Johannesburg, South Africa. My journey began in 2014 during high school, initially focusing on automotive photography alongside my cousin before transitioning to portraiture. In 2016, during my first year at university, I joined the university newspaper and documented the historic "Fees Must Fall" protests, which taught me the profound importance of storytelling through photography.

My work spans multiple genres including documentary, portraiture, fashion, fine art, and architecture photography. I believe in the power of visual storytelling to capture moments, emotions, and narratives that words cannot express.
