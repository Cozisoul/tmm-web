What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

How We Use Cookies

Thapelo Madiba Masebe's photography portfolio uses cookies to:

  • Remember your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns (anonymously)
  • Ensure website security and prevent fraud

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration Essential
Theme Preference Remembers your light/dark mode choice 1 year No
Analytics Helps us understand how visitors use the site 2 years No
Security Protects against malicious attacks Session Yes
Performance Improves website loading speed 1 month Yes

Your Cookie Choices

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them. However, this may affect website functionality.
  • Cookie Banner: When you first visit our site, you can choose which cookies to accept.
  • Theme Toggle: You can change your theme preference at any time using the theme toggle button.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts: For loading web fonts (no tracking)
  • Analytics: For understanding website usage (anonymized data only)

Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Any data collected through cookies is:

  • Used only for the purposes stated above
  • Stored securely and not shared with third parties
  • Anonymized where possible
  • Subject to our Privacy Policy
